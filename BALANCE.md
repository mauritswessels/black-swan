# Balance report

Generated by `npm run playtest`. Each strategy played the same 500 games (seeds 1 to 500), 20 quarters each, so the comparison is fair.

**Score:** each quarter earns 100 * e^(-loss / 3) points, and the term score is the average over 20 quarters. Quarters lost to a game over earn 0.
"Avg inflation miss" is the average distance of headline inflation from the centre of the target.

## Strategies

- **Do nothing:** Never moves the rate.
- **Random:** Moves the rate by a random step between -0.5 and +0.5.
- **Overly hawkish:** Taylor rule plus 1 point.
- **Overly dovish:** Taylor rule minus 1 point.
- **Taylor rule:** Follows the Taylor rule on the latest published headline inflation and output gap.
- **Smart bot:** Rule plus judgment: looks through energy, averages first estimates with revised data, moves gradually, leans against a soaring thaler.
